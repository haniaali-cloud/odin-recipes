Lasagna

Description

Lasagna combines a hearty tomato and meat ragu with a creamy cheese filling, layered sequentially in a baking dish, and baked until bubbly and golden brown.

Ingredients

  • Ground Beef
  • Marinara
  • Lasagna noodles
  • Cheese
  • Italian Seasoning, salt, and pepper

Steps

  1. Cook the meat: Brown the ground beef in a pan, then stir in the marinara sauce and let it simmer.
  2. Layer the dish: Spread a thin layer of meat sauce in a baking pan, add noodles, top with cheese mixture and meat sauce, and repeat the layers.
  3. Add final cheese: Top the final layer of noodles with the remaining meat sauce and a heavy layer of mozzarella and Parmesan cheese.
  4. Bake: Cover with foil and bake at 375°F (190°C) for 30 minutes, then uncover and bake for 15 more minutes until golden and bubbly.
